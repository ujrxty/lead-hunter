# Lead Hunter — Instagram reel script

Total target: **30-45 seconds** (Reels sweet spot).
Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) — record your screen zoomed in on relevant parts and crop.

## Recording setup
- **OBS Studio** (free) or **ScreenPal** for capture
- Set browser zoom to **125-150%** so text reads on mobile
- Hide bookmarks bar, personal tabs — clean chrome
- Use **1440×900** window size for consistent crops
- Record at **60fps** — reels feel snappier

## Aesthetic
- Zero cursor jitter — plan clicks, don't hunt
- Cut clip-to-clip fast, no lingering shots
- **No captions in the video** — burn them in during edit for stops/pops

## Shot list (each shot 2-4s)

### HOOK (0-3s) — grab attention
**Shot 1** · Full app open on the Signal tab, dark UI, lime accents visible.
> Text overlay: **"I built an AI hunter for Upwork clients"**

### PROBLEM (3-6s) — the pain
**Shot 2** · Zoom on a bunch of generic Upwork job cards, mouse scrolling fast, blur.
> Text overlay: **"Every post looks the same"**

### MECHANIC 1 — Scraping (6-11s)
**Shot 3** · Click Run, watch the depth selector, cards start populating.
> Text overlay: **"Scrapes hundreds of jobs"**

### MECHANIC 2 — Company detection (11-16s)
**Shot 4** · Toggle "Company signals only" — list collapses from 108 → 5.
> Text overlay: **"Finds the ones with real companies"** (highlight lime badge)

### MECHANIC 3 — Enrichment (16-22s)
**Shot 5** · Click enrich icon on a hot lead card. Cut to Leads tab showing website + email pulled.
> Text overlay: **"Pulls their website + email"**

### MECHANIC 4 — Proposal (22-30s)
**Shot 6** · Click the ✨ proposal icon. Modal opens, "Generating…" spinner.
> Text overlay: **"Then writes the proposal for you"**
**Shot 7** · Cut to fully generated proposal. Slow scroll through sections.

### CLOSER (30-35s) — CTA
**Shot 8** · Close modal, back to Signal tab wide shot, crosshair logo prominent.
> Text overlay: **"Free & open source"**
> Sub-text: **"github.com/ujrxty/lead-hunter"**

## Caption ideas for the post

**Short + punchy:**
> Built an AI hunter for Upwork clients.
>
> - Scrapes past Cloudflare
> - Detects real companies (rejects the "LLMs / RAG" false positives)
> - Enriches with website + email
> - Drafts personalised proposals with Groq
>
> Free & open source. Link in bio.

**Longer, story-first:**
> Every Upwork post looks the same. Same buzzwords, same 500 applicants.
>
> So I built Lead Hunter — it flags the jobs where clients name their actual company (the ones actually worth applying to), enriches them with contact info, and drafts personalised proposals with AI.
>
> All local, MIT licensed. github.com/ujrxty/lead-hunter
